Traditional Chinese ink-and-colour painting in the style of a Ming dynasty literati album leaf, wide panoramic 16:9 banner composition of the Forbidden City in Beijing. The upper third of the picture is open, pale and calm: soft mist and faint distant Western Hills suggested with a few dry brushstrokes, leaving generous empty negative space for a title. The architecture sits in the lower two thirds: golden-yellow glazed tile roofs with upturned eaves, deep vermilion palace walls, an ornate corner tower with layered dougong brackets, a multi-tiered paifang gate, ancient cypress and pine trees, white marble balustrades and a carved stone bridge, a wide calm moat mirroring the buildings. Aged xuan-paper texture with faint fibre grain, muted restrained palette of ink black, ochre, vermilion and antique gold, soft diffused daylight, quiet and elegant, gongbi line work combined with xieyi washes, subtle ink bleed at the edges.
