Traditional Chinese ink-and-colour painting in the style of a Ming dynasty literati album leaf, wide panoramic 16:9 composition of the Forbidden City in Beijing. Golden-yellow glazed tile roofs with upturned eaves, deep vermilion palace walls, an ornate corner tower with layered dougong brackets, white marble balustrades and a carved stone bridge, a multi-tiered paifang gate, ancient cypress and pine trees, a wide calm moat mirroring the architecture. Distant misty Western Hills suggested with sparse dry brushwork. The middle band of the picture is left as open, pale, misty negative space, calm and uncluttered, so that text can be placed there later. Aged xuan-paper texture with faint fibre grain, muted restrained palette of ink black, ochre, vermilion and antique gold, soft diffused daylight, elegant and quiet, gongbi line work combined with xieyi washes, subtle ink bleed at the edges.
